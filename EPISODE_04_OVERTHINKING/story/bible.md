## Key Sets

1. **cafe_street (`dusk` / `golden`):**
   - An outdoor café table on a quiet city sidewalk.
   - Warm golden sunset glow from the left, deep violet street shadows, distant streetlamp.
   - Key light: sunset horizon and café glass (`rim: dir [-1, -0.2]`).

2. **bedroom_night (`midnight` / `dusk`):**
   - Alex's dark bedroom at 2:14 AM.
   - Low-angle bed, window blinds casting pale cyan moonlight slats across the floor.
   - Digital alarm clock glowing soft amber on the nightstand.
   - Floating thought particles and conversational ghosts in the air.

3. **thought_loop (`void` / `neon` / `midnight`):**
   - Deep charcoal-navy mental space with subtle floor grid lines.
   - Giant luminous dominoes tipping over in slow motion.
   - Circular luminous paths with arrows feeding back into themselves without an exit.

4. **brain_network (`blueprint` / `dusk`):**
   - Stylized profile cross-section of the human head and brain.
   - The Default Mode Network (mPFC, Posterior Cingulate, Angular Gyrus) glowing in electric cyan and gold.
   - Active neural pulses flowing between regions during daydreaming and self-reflection.

5. **meeting_room (`dusk`):**
   - Surreal distortion where the bed extends into an endless corporate boardroom table.
   - Shadow figures sitting under harsh fluorescent cones, holding papers with no agenda.

6. **turning_point (`dusk` / `golden`):**
   - Early dawn exterior: empty quiet park path with amber streetlights, cool mist.
   - Alex writing a thought into a small pocket notebook, stepping out, taking a deep breath of fresh air.

## Visual Motifs & Pacing

- **The Freeze / Pause:** When Alex says the wrong word, time slows; particles hang in the air.
- **The Domino Chain:** Unfolding thoughts visualized as sleek minimalist vector dominoes tumbling forward.
- **Blueprint Overlays:** Technical neuroscience concepts visualized on deep navy blueprint grids with gold and electric cyan vectors.
- **Letterboxing:** `wide` (2:1) standard cinematic bars; `scope` (2.39:1) for bedroom insomnia close-ups, time freezes, and title cards.
